A Little Promise ❤️

Triveni, I don't know what the future is going to look like, but there is one thing I know for sure...

We may get angry. We may misunderstand each other. We may even stop talking for a while.

But I hope we never stop caring. ❤️

❤️ I promise I'll always value our friendship.

❤️ I promise I'll remember the little moments we shared.

❤️ I promise I'll be there whenever you need a friend.

❤️ I promise I'll listen when you want to talk.

❤️ I promise I'll always wish the best for you.
