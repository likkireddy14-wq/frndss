02 — THE WORDS I COULDN'T SAY

💌

Dear Triveni...

I don't really know where to start.

Maybe with a simple I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the fight. I'm sorry for the things that hurt you. I'm sorry that something that should have been small became something that came between us.

I could sit here and explain my side of everything... but honestly, I don't want to prove that I was right.

Because even if I was right about something, losing you would never feel like winning.

The truth is...

I miss you.

I miss talking to you.

I miss laughing with you.

I miss those random conversations that didn't even have a reason.

I miss having you around.

It's strange how you don't realize how much someone has become a part of your everyday life until suddenly they're not there anymore.

And that's when I realized something I should have realized a long time ago.

You matter to me. A lot. ❤️

Triveni, our friendship isn't just a collection of conversations.

It's the memories. The laughter. The stupid jokes. The little moments. The times we understood each other without even having to explain.

And I don't want one fight to become the last chapter of our story.

I want there to be more.

More conversations. More laughter. More stupid moments. More memories.

I want to see you. 🥺

Not through a screen.

Not through old pictures.

I want to actually meet you.

Sit with you.

Talk to you.

And hopefully laugh together like we used to.

So please... when you're ready, let's meet soon. ❤️

“

If I could go back...

I'd choose one more conversation with you over a hundred arguments about who was right.

I'd choose your smile over my ego.

I'd choose our friendship again. ❤️

I don't know what you're feeling right now.

Maybe you're still angry. Maybe you're hurt. Maybe you need time.

And that's okay.

I'm not asking you to forget everything instantly.

I'm just asking you not to forget us.

Because no matter how many people come into our lives, there will always be certain people whose place can never really be replaced.

And you're one of those people for me. ❤️

I don't want perfection.

I just want us to be okay again.

I want to hear your voice.

I want to see you smile.

I want to make new memories instead of only looking at the old ones.

No matter how far we are...

No matter how angry we get...

No matter how complicated things become...

You'll always have me.

I promise. 🤞❤️

I miss you, Triveni.
I hope we meet soon.

— Your friend, always 🌷
